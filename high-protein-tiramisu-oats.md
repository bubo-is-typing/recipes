# High-Protein Tiramisu Oats

## Ingredients

- 50 g rolled oats
- 175 g vanilla skyr, or plain skyr mixed with 3–6 g flavour powder
- 30 g ESN Flexpresso Dark Choc. Mocha
- 15 g chia seeds
- 7 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup coffee, cooled

## Method

1. Stir together the rolled oats, chia seeds, Flexpresso and cooled coffee.
2. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours, preferably overnight, to thicken.
3. Top with the skyr, sift over the cocoa powder and serve.
