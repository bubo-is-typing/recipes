# Magnum-Style Chocolate Chia Pudding

## Ingredients

### Chocolate chia pudding

- 200 g thick Greek yoghurt or skyr
- 10 g cocoa powder
- 15 g chocolate protein powder (optional)
- 30 g chia seeds
- A splash of vanilla extract
- A pinch of salt
- Syrup to taste (the creator uses maple syrup)
- A little milk, if needed to loosen the mixture (optional)

### Yoghurt layer

- 70 g yoghurt
- 1 tsp maple syrup

### Chocolate shell

- 20–25 g dark chocolate, melted

### To serve (optional)

- Berries

## Method

1. Mix the 200 g yoghurt or skyr, cocoa powder, optional protein powder, chia seeds, vanilla, salt and syrup. Add a little milk only if you prefer a looser pudding.
2. Refrigerate the chia mixture for **2–3 hours** to thicken.
3. Spoon the pudding into a small clear jar or serving bowl.
4. Stir together the 70 g yoghurt and 1 tsp maple syrup. Spread this mixture evenly over the chia pudding.
5. Melt the dark chocolate and spread or drizzle it over the yoghurt to form a thin, continuous top layer.
6. Refrigerate **overnight**, until the chocolate has set into a shell. Crack the shell with a spoon to eat. Serve with berries if desired.

## Notes

- The creator calls this a breakfast inspired by a Magnum ice-cream bar and explicitly says it will **not** taste like an actual Magnum. It is a chilled chia pudding, not ice cream.
