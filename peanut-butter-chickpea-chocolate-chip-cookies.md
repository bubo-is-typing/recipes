# Peanut Butter Chickpea Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Ingredients

- 600 g chickpeas, drained
- 40 g smooth peanut butter
- 20 g honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 30 g Noshu chocolate chips

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C / 350°F and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Add the drained chickpeas, peanut butter, honey, vanilla extract and baking powder to a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth and thick, with a cookie-dough consistency.
3. Fold the chocolate chips through the dough.
4. Divide the mixture into 12 cookies on the lined tray. Shape and slightly flatten each one.
5. Bake for 15–20 minutes, or until lightly golden on top. Let the cookies cool before eating; the creator says they firm up a little as they cool.
