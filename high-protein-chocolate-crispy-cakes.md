# High-Protein Chocolate Crispy Cakes

## Ingredients

- 180 g chocolate or chocolate chips (about 1 cup)
- 200 g smooth peanut butter (about 3/4 cup)
- 270 g high-protein pea or soy crisps (about 3 cups; see Notes)
- Pinch of sea salt (optional)

## Method

1. Line a 12-cup muffin tray with paper cases, or use a silicone muffin tray.
2. Melt the chocolate gently in the microwave in 20–30-second bursts, stirring after each burst.
3. Stir in the peanut butter until smooth. If the peanut butter is too stiff to mix easily, warm it separately for about 20 seconds first.
4. Add the high-protein crisps and stir gently until evenly coated.
5. Divide the mixture among the 12 muffin cups and press each portion down firmly so the cakes hold together.
6. Freeze for about 1 hour, or until set.
7. Store chilled or frozen. If serving straight from the freezer, let the cakes soften for about 10 minutes first.

## Notes

- Makes 12 cakes.
- The creator recommends measuring the protein crisps by volume because brands vary in weight.
- The peanut butter needs to be runny enough to coat the crisps evenly.
- Store the cakes in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks or in the freezer for up to 3 months.
- The creator reports approximately 270 kcal, 18 g protein and 2–3 g fibre per cake; these values have not been independently verified.
