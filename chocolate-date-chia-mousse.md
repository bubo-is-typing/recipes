# High-Protein Chocolate Date Chia Mousse

*Adam Hoad’s measured chocolate-date mousse recipe, demonstrated without measured quantities in James White’s short. Makes 3 servings; chill for at least 2 hours.*

## Ingredients

- 150 g dates
- 60 g chia seeds
- 30 g cocoa powder
- 400 g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 150 ml milk
- 1 scoop whey protein powder (scoop size not specified)
- 5 g vanilla extract
- A pinch of salt
- Optional toppings shown by James: raspberries, coconut flakes or chocolate pieces (amounts not specified)

## Method

1. Remove any pits from the dates. Put the dates, cocoa powder, Greek yoghurt, milk, whey protein powder, vanilla extract and salt in a blender. Blend until smooth.
2. Stir in the chia seeds so they are evenly distributed. Divide the mixture among three small serving pots.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, until set. Serve chilled, plain or with one of James’s optional toppings.
