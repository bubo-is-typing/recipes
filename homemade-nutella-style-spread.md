# Five-Ingredient Homemade Nutella-Style Spread

*A homemade hazelnut, cocoa and date spread demonstrated by FirstTakeHadrian, who credits @susan.healthy as the original inspiration.*

## Ingredients

### Spread

- 2 cups roasted hazelnuts (volume measure; the source gives no weight)
- 4 dates, softened
- 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- A little maple syrup (quantity not specified)
- A pinch of salt

### For serving, optional

- Toasted sourdough bread, as desired

## Method

1. Put the roasted hazelnuts into a blender. Blend for about 3 minutes, until they break down into a smooth, creamy nut butter.
2. Add the softened dates, cocoa powder, a little maple syrup and a pinch of salt.
3. Blend again until evenly combined and spreadable. The description calls for a completely smooth, glossy mixture; the demonstrated finished spread is very dark and visibly thicker and grainier than commercial Nutella.
4. Spread over toasted sourdough to serve, if desired.
