# Creamy Boursin Chicken Orzo Bake

*Makes 4 servings.*

## Ingredients

- 300 g cooked rotisserie chicken
- 180 g dry orzo
- 800 g cherry tomatoes
- 400 g courgette, chopped
- 125 g Boursin cheese
- 60 g grated Parmesan
- A handful of fresh basil
- Chilli flakes
- Oregano
- Salt and black pepper
- 4 ml olive oil spray

## Method

1. Place the Boursin in the centre of a baking tray or ovenproof dish. Arrange the cherry tomatoes and chopped courgette around it.
2. Season with chilli flakes, oregano, salt and black pepper, then give everything a couple of sprays of olive oil.
3. Bake in a preheated fan oven at 180°C for 35 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, cook the orzo according to the packet instructions.
5. Remove the tray from the oven and stir everything together until the Boursin forms a creamy sauce. Add the cooked orzo, basil and rotisserie chicken, then mix well.
6. Top with the grated Parmesan and return to the oven for 5 minutes, until the cheese has melted.
