# Sprite Black Tea with Lemon

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle Sprite
- 2 black tea bags
- Ice
- A small squeeze of lemon juice

## Method

1. Open the bottle of Sprite and carefully push both black tea bags into the bottle.
2. Close the bottle securely, stand it upside down in a cup and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
3. Fill a glass with ice and pour over the chilled tea-infused Sprite.
4. Add a small squeeze of lemon juice and serve immediately.

## Notes

- The source does not specify the bottle size, variety of black tea, quantity of ice or exact amount of lemon juice.
- Open and invert the carbonated bottle carefully to avoid spills.
- The creator describes this as a viral drink from Hong Kong and found it more tea-forward and refreshing than expected.
