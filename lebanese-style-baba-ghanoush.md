# Lebanese-Style Baba Ghanoush

*Chef Mike’s hand-mashed eggplant and tahini dip, learned from his mother and finished with olive oil.*

## Ingredients

### Dip

- 1 large eggplant
- 2–3 garlic cloves, smashed or grated
- ½ cup tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt, to taste
- Cold water, for peeling the roasted eggplant

### To finish and serve

- Olive oil, a generous amount for topping (quantity not specified)
- Bread, for dipping (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. Roast the whole eggplant over an open stove flame or on a grill, turning it until the skin is completely charred and splitting and the flesh has softened.
2. Put the roasted eggplant in a bowl, add cold water and peel away the burned skin. Drain the water and set the peeled eggplant aside.
3. Put the garlic and a sprinkle of salt into a mixing bowl. Crush together with a pestle or potato masher.
4. Remove the eggplant stem, add the flesh to the bowl and mash it into small pieces by hand. Chef Mike specifically advises against using a food processor, saying it makes the mixture too watery.
5. Add another sprinkle of salt, the lemon juice and the tahini. Continue mashing until everything is combined and mostly creamy, retaining the rustic texture.
6. Spoon into a serving dish, top generously with olive oil and serve with bread for dipping.
