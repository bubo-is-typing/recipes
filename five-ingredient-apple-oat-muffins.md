# Five-Ingredient Apple Oat Muffins

## Ingredients

- 2 apples, grated (about 200 g according to the video description)
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (about 160 g according to the description)
- 2 large eggs
- About 30 g honey, the amount the tester says he used; see the honey note below
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (approximately 177°C) and line a muffin tin with paper cases. The video description says the full recipe makes 12 muffins.
2. Grate the apples with a box grater. In a bowl, mix the grated apple, oats, eggs, honey and cinnamon thoroughly to form a cohesive, chunky batter.
3. Spoon the batter into the lined muffin cups, dividing it evenly.
4. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, until the muffins are set, golden and baked through. Let them cool slightly before serving.

## Notes

- **Honey discrepancy:** The spoken instructions in the Short call for ¼ cup raw honey, but FirstTakeHadrian's written description says he used only about 30 g (or less) and found the muffins sweet enough. These are **alternative amounts, not equivalent conversions**; the ingredient list follows his reported test batch.
- The video description lists only four ingredients, but the narration explicitly adds 1 teaspoon of cinnamon as the fifth.
- Makes 12 muffins **according to the description**. The exact apple varieties, whether to peel them, oven mode and cooling time are not specified.
- The tester reports approximately 90 calories, 3 g protein, 13 g carbohydrate and 2 g fat per muffin; these figures are not independently verified and may vary with honey quantity and actual yield.
