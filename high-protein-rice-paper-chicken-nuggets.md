# High-Protein Rice-Paper Chicken Nuggets

## Ingredients

### Nuggets

- 250 g chicken breast
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 egg
- Rice paper wrappers, as needed (see Notes)
- A little oil for the filling and for spraying the wrapped nuggets; quantity not specified

### Optional Buffalo-style coating

- Butter, quantity not specified
- Buffalo Tabasco sauce, quantity not specified

## Method

1. Put the chicken breast in a food processor with the smoked paprika, cayenne, garlic powder, salt, egg and a little oil. Blend to a smooth, cohesive mixture.
2. Briefly wet both sides of a rice paper wrapper. Lay it on a board and spread the chicken mixture in an even line along the centre.
3. Fold the wrapper over the filling and roll it into a compact log, spreading the filling evenly as you go. Cut the filled log into nugget-sized pieces. Repeat with the remaining mixture and wrappers.
4. Arrange the pieces in an air-fryer basket and lightly spray them with oil. Air-fry at **200°C for 12 minutes**, until cooked through and crisp.
5. Transfer the nuggets to a wire rack and let them cool briefly so the wrappers remain crisp. Serve plain or with a sauce.
6. For the optional Buffalo-style version, melt some butter with Buffalo Tabasco sauce and toss the cooked nuggets in the mixture.
