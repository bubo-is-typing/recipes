# Chocolate Biscoff Protein Bowl

## Ingredients

### Chocolate skyr layer

- 300 g thick fat-free skyr
- 40 g chocolate-flavoured whey protein concentrate
- 10 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- About 2 g cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

### Biscuit layer

- 4 petit-beurre-style tea biscuits (20 g total)

### Cookie skyr layer

- 200 g thick fat-free skyr
- 20 g cookie-flavoured plant-based protein
- 0–60 ml unsweetened almond milk
- Cinnamon, to taste

### Divider and topping

- 2 Lotus Biscoff biscuits (about 15.5 g total)
- 50 g milk chocolate
- 4 g coconut oil
- 3–4 g desiccated coconut
- 5–7 g tahini
- 6 blueberries (approximately 12 g)

## Method

1. Mix the skyr, chocolate whey protein, cocoa, cinnamon and salt very well for 2–3 minutes. Spread evenly across the dish.
2. Place the four petit-beurre biscuits over the chocolate layer.
3. Mix the skyr, cookie-flavoured plant protein and cinnamon for the second layer. Start with no more than 0–20 ml almond milk, adding more only as needed to produce a thick, spreadable mixture. Spread it over the biscuit layer.
4. Stand the two Biscoff biscuits end-to-end on their long edges, diagonally from the midpoint of one side towards the opposite corner, creating a small triangular section.
5. Microwave the milk chocolate and coconut oil in short intervals, stirring after each one, until smooth. Spread it over the larger section outside the Biscoff divider.
6. Sprinkle the desiccated coconut along one edge.
7. Make six small drops using 2–3 g of the tahini in the exposed section, then place one blueberry on each drop. Drizzle the remaining tahini along the Biscoff divider.
8. Refrigerate until the chocolate has fully set.

## Notes

- The creator uses a 0.8-litre dish measuring 19 × 14 × 5 cm externally and 17 × 12 × 4 cm internally.
- Approximate macros for the whole dish: 1,147 calories, 114 g protein, 87 g carbohydrates and 40 g fat. Half provides approximately 573 calories and 57 g protein; one-third provides approximately 382 calories and 38 g protein.
- Maria cookies or plain tea cookies are suggested as US alternatives to petit-beurre biscuits; Rich Tea biscuits are suggested in the UK.
- Plant protein can thicken the mixture considerably. The creator needed all 60 ml milk, but recommends adding it gradually. When using whey instead, begin without milk; if the mixture remains too thin, mix in about 0.3 g (roughly ⅛ tsp) xanthan gum.
- The source does not specify an exact refrigeration time.
