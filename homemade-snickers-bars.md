# Homemade Snickers Bars

## Ingredients

### Creamy base

- 130 g cashews
- 240 g coconut cream
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- Pinch of fleur de sel

### Peanut caramel

- 260 g peanut butter
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Topping and coating

- Roasted peanuts, as needed
- Dark chocolate (70% cocoa or higher), as needed
- Fleur de sel, for finishing

## Method

1. Blend the cashews, coconut cream, maple syrup and salt until completely smooth and creamy.
2. Pour the mixture into a baking-paper-lined tray and freeze until firm.
3. Mix the peanut butter, maple syrup and vanilla until smooth and silky.
4. Spread the caramel over the frozen base and scatter roasted peanuts on top.
5. Freeze again until solid enough to cut.
6. Cut into bars.
7. Dip each bar in melted dark chocolate and finish with fleur de sel.
8. Chill until the chocolate has set.

- The creator doubled the recipe for the video, so the bars shown are larger than this batch would normally make.
