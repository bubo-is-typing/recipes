# High-Protein Blueberry Lemon Cheesecake

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225 g) cottage cheese
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 large egg
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/4 cup sweetener
- Blueberries, to taste
- Butter or neutral oil, for greasing

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. Blend the cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, egg, lemon juice and sweetener until completely smooth.
3. Lightly grease two ramekins.
4. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared ramekins.
5. Add blueberries to each ramekin, gently pressing some into the batter.
6. Bake for about 45 minutes, or until the cheesecakes are set.
7. Let them cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.

## Notes

- The source does not specify the quantity of blueberries or the type of sweetener; use your preferred granulated sweetener and add blueberries to taste.
- The recipe yields two individual ramekin cheesecakes.
- Chilling the cheesecakes for at least 2 hours gives them the smoothest, creamiest texture.
- Keep covered in the refrigerator and serve chilled.
