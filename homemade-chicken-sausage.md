# Homemade Chicken Sausage

*Simmer for about 1 hour, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.*

## Ingredients

- Chicken legs (amount not specified), or 1 whole chicken
- 1 large carrot
- 1 large onion
- 2 bay leaves
- A few peppercorns
- Salt
- Water for simmering; reserve some of the resulting broth
- A little beetroot juice, for colour
- Seasonings of your choice
- Instant powdered gelatin (amount not specified)

## Method

1. Put the chicken, carrot, onion, bay leaves, peppercorns and salt in a pot with water. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour, until the chicken is cooked through and very tender.
2. Lift out the chicken, pull the meat off the bones and reserve some broth.
3. Put the meat in a blender with the seasonings. Stir the gelatin into the beetroot juice as suggested in the narration and add it to the blender. Blend until very smooth, adding a little broth at a time. Rooxx suggests an immersion blender as an alternative.
4. Pour into a tall container and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
5. Slip a skewer down the inside edge of the container to let air in, then unmould and slice. If a dark layer of beetroot juice has separated, trim it off as Rooxx does in the clip.
