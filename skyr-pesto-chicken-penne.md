# Creamy Skyr-Pesto Penne with Chicken

*Gabel und Kompass’s version of Arda Saatçi’s skyr-and-pesto pasta, with seasoned chicken and Parmesan. Makes 1 serving.*

## Ingredients

- 60 g penne
- 200 g plain skyr
- 1 tbsp green pesto
- 150 g chicken breast
- Parmesan, for mixing into the sauce and finishing (amount not specified)
- Salt (amount not specified)
- Garlic powder (amount not specified)
- Onion powder (amount not specified)
- Smoked paprika (amount not specified)
- A splash of pasta cooking water (amount not specified)

## Method

1. Cook the penne according to its packet instructions. Reserve a little of the cooking water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, cut the chicken breast into small pieces. Season with salt, garlic powder, onion powder and smoked paprika. Fry in a pan until browned and cooked through. **Food-safety addition:** check the thickest piece reaches 74°C with a food thermometer; the creator does not give a temperature or frying time.
3. Stir the skyr, pesto and some Parmesan together. Toss the cooked penne and chicken through this mixture off the heat. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if needed to make the sauce creamy.
4. Finish with more Parmesan and serve.
