# Frozen Peanut Butter Yogurt Bites

## Ingredients

### Creamy filling

- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/3 cup honey

### Chocolate shell and topping

- 1 cup dark chocolate (70% cocoa or higher)
- Fleur de sel, for topping (optional)
- Crushed peanuts, for topping (optional)

## Method

1. Mix the Greek yogurt, peanut butter and honey until smooth and creamy.
2. Scoop small mounds onto a baking-paper-lined tray.
3. Freeze for 1 hour, or until completely firm.
4. Melt the dark chocolate gently.
5. Dip each frozen bite into the chocolate until fully coated.
6. Before the shell sets, sprinkle with fleur de sel and crushed peanuts if desired.
7. Return the bites to the freezer for 20–30 minutes, until the chocolate is hard.
8. Serve frozen or allow them to soften briefly before eating.

## Notes

- The source does not specify the yield; it depends on the size of the scoops.
- Keep the bites frozen in an airtight container. Let them stand at room temperature for a few minutes if the centre is too firm.
