# Date Caramel Blended Oats

## Ingredients

### Date caramel

- 1⅔ cups dried pitted dates (about 250 g)
- 2 cups boiling water
- ⅛ tsp salt (optional)

### Blended oats

- 2 cups rolled oats (about 180 g)
- 1 cup reserved date-soaking water
- 1 cup plain unsweetened soy or coconut yoghurt (about 225 g)
- 2 tbsp almond butter (about 45 g; optional)
- 2 tbsp prepared date caramel
- 3 tbsp chia seeds (about 30 g)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ⅛ tsp salt

### Topping

- 4 tbsp prepared date caramel
- 100 g dark chocolate, melted

## Method

1. Put the dates in a medium heatproof bowl, pour over the boiling water and soak for 20 minutes.
2. Drain the dates, reserving the soaking water. Blend the softened dates with 1 tablespoon of the reserved water until smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides and adding only as much extra water as needed for a thick caramel. Mix in the optional salt.
3. Add the rolled oats, 1 cup reserved date-soaking water, yoghurt, optional almond butter, 2 tablespoons date caramel, chia seeds, vanilla and salt to a high-powered blender.
4. Blend on high for 30–60 seconds, until thick and smooth like a pourable batter.
5. Divide evenly between four jars or containers and smooth the tops.
6. Spoon 1 tablespoon date caramel over each portion, then drizzle each with one-quarter of the melted dark chocolate.
7. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, until chilled and set. Break through the chocolate top to serve.

## Notes

- Makes 4 servings. The creator reports approximately 500 kcal, 67 g carbohydrates, 13 g protein, 22 g fat, 13 g fibre and 24 g sugar per serving; these values have not been independently verified.
- The date caramel recipe makes approximately one 10 fl oz (300 ml) jar, so there may be caramel left after measuring the 6 tablespoons needed for the oats.
- If the reserved date-soaking water is unavailable, the creator suggests replacing it with 1 cup boiling water mixed with 2 tablespoons maple syrup.
- Keep the blended oats refrigerated in sealed jars or airtight containers for up to 4 days. Keep leftover date caramel refrigerated for up to 1 week.
