# Peanut Butter Oat Cups

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 banana, sliced
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- Extra natural peanut butter, for drizzling
- Dark chocolate, for topping (optional)

## Method

1. Mix the rolled oats, peanut butter and honey or maple syrup until sticky and well combined.
2. Press the mixture firmly into small muffin cups, shaping it around the sides to leave a hollow centre.
3. Place a few banana slices into each oat cup.
4. Spoon Greek yogurt over the banana layer.
5. Drizzle with peanut butter and optionally add a little dark chocolate.
6. Refrigerate until firm and set.
7. Carefully remove the cups from the mould and serve.

## Notes

- The source does not specify the yield or exact chilling time.
- Because the filling contains banana and Greek yogurt, keep the cups refrigerated.
