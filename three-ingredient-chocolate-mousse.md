# Three-Ingredient Chocolate Mousse

*Makes 1 large serving. Chill the coconut milk overnight, then chill the mousse for 3 hours.*

## Ingredients

- 1 can (400 ml) low-fat coconut milk
- 2 tbsp (10 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) sugar-free maple syrup

## Method

1. Refrigerate the can of coconut milk overnight so the cream separates and firms up.
2. Tip both the cream and the liquid into a mixing bowl. Whisk vigorously until thick and fluffy.
3. Gently fold or whisk in the cocoa powder and syrup until smooth.
4. Refrigerate for 3 hours, until set.

## Notes

- West’s written recipe calls for both parts of the coconut milk. The video says he chilled his finished batch for 3 hours.
- West’s estimate for the whole batch: 290 kcal, 6 g protein, 21 g carbohydrates, 21.5 g fat and 3 g fibre. These figures were not independently verified.
- The AI-generated illustration shows a serving of mousse, not West’s actual batch.
