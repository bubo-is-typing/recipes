# Fudge Brownie Cheesecake Bars

## Ingredients

### Brownie base

- 150 g oat flour
- 30 g cocoa powder
- 30 g sugar-free maple syrup
- 20 ml coconut oil
- 3 tbsp unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Cheesecake layer

- 250 g light cream cheese
- 10 g powdered sweetener
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- A squeeze of lemon juice

### Chocolate topping

- 40 g cocoa powder
- 20 g sugar-free maple syrup
- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 2 tbsp unsweetened almond milk

## Method

1. Line a small tray or glass dish with baking paper.
2. Mix the oat flour, cocoa powder, maple syrup, coconut oil, almond milk and vanilla until evenly combined.
3. Press the brownie mixture firmly into the lined tray, smooth the surface and freeze for 10 minutes.
4. Beat the light cream cheese, powdered sweetener, vanilla and lemon juice with a hand mixer until smooth.
5. Spread the cheesecake mixture evenly over the brownie base and freeze for 45 minutes.
6. Mix the cocoa powder, maple syrup, coconut oil and almond milk into a thick, fudgy sauce.
7. Spread the chocolate sauce over the cheesecake layer and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
8. Cut into 6 bars and serve chilled.

## Notes

- The creator reports 279 kcal, 8 g protein, 17 g fat and 19 g carbohydrates per bar when the batch is cut into 6; these values have not been independently verified.
- The written description and spoken video differ on a few quantities. This recipe follows the creator’s written quantities, except that the cheesecake vanilla is listed as 1 tsp to match the spoken instruction.
- Keep the bars refrigerated in an airtight container; freeze briefly before cutting if the layers have softened.
