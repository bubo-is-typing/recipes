# Epicurious Fluffy Pancakes

*Serves 4. The supplied recipe card lists 15 minutes preparation, 15 minutes cooking and 30 minutes total.*

## Ingredients

### Dry ingredients

- 375 g all-purpose flour
- 50 g sugar
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1½ tsp baking soda

### Wet ingredients

- 540 ml milk
- 60 ml vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 60 ml apple cider vinegar
- 3 eggs

## Method

1. Mix all the dry ingredients together.
2. Mix all the wet ingredients together separately.
3. Combine the wet and dry mixtures, stirring only very lightly until just combined. Do not overmix: the card warns that excessive mixing breaks the bubbles that have formed.
4. Leave the batter undisturbed. The card does not specify a resting time.
5. Heat a pan. The card does not specify the heat setting or whether to grease the pan.
6. Gently take batter for each pancake, disturbing the remaining batter as little as possible. Cook the pancakes in the heated pan; the card does not give a portion size, flipping cue or cooking time per side.
