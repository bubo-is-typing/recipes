# High-Protein Frozen Yogurt Bars

## Ingredients

### Yogurt base

- 1 1/2 cups non-fat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 scoop French vanilla whey protein powder
- 1 tbsp raw honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Toppings

- Fresh strawberries, chopped, to taste
- Walnuts, crushed, to taste
- Dark chocolate, melted, for drizzling, to taste

## Method

1. Stir the Greek yogurt, protein powder, honey and vanilla extract together until smooth.
2. Line a baking sheet or tray with baking paper.
3. Pour the yogurt mixture onto the prepared tray and spread it into an even rectangle, keeping it thick enough to cut into bars.
4. Scatter over the chopped strawberries and crushed walnuts, then drizzle with melted dark chocolate.
5. Freeze for at least 1 1/2 hours, or until completely firm.
6. Cut or break into four bars and serve frozen.

## Notes

- The creator reports 207 kcal, 34 g protein, 1 g fat and 17 g carbohydrates for a four-bar serving; the toppings are excluded from those figures and the values have not been independently verified.
- The source does not specify quantities for the strawberries, walnuts or chocolate.
- Keep the bars frozen in an airtight container. Let them soften briefly at room temperature if they are too firm to bite.
