# Crispy Tomato & Olive Oil Bread

## Ingredients

### For the topping

- 1 large tomato
- 1 small red onion
- 1 garlic clove
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 2 tbsp pine nuts
- Olive oil
- Balsamic vinegar
- Salt
- Black pepper

### To serve

- Fresh bread

## Method

1. Mix the olive oil and balsamic vinegar in a bowl.
2. Finely grate the garlic, very finely dice the red onion and finely chop the parsley. Add everything to the oil and balsamic mixture.
3. Coarsely grate the tomato and add it to the bowl.
4. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan until golden, then fold them into the tomato mixture.
5. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper, and mix well.
6. Cut the bread into slices and toast until crisp.
7. Spoon the tomato mixture generously over the crisp bread and serve immediately.
