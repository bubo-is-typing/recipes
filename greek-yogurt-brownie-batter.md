# Greek Yogurt Brownie Batter

## Ingredients

- 120 g fat-free Greek yoghurt, or full-fat Greek yoghurt
- 20 g oat flour
- 10 g cocoa powder
- 15 g nut butter (the creator uses almond butter)
- Liquid sweetener, to taste (the creator uses maple syrup)
- Pinch of salt
- Splash of vanilla extract
- 15 g vegan chocolate protein powder (optional)
- 10–15 g chocolate shavings, divided
- 30–40 milk or water (unit not specified by the creator)

## Method

1. Mix the Greek yoghurt, oat flour, cocoa powder, nut butter, liquid sweetener, salt and vanilla extract together. Add the optional protein powder if using.
2. Add the milk or water gradually, using only enough to form a thick brownie-batter consistency.
3. Chill the mixture in the refrigerator for approximately 30 minutes.
4. Mix in some of the chocolate shavings.
5. Scoop into a serving bowl, top with the remaining chocolate shavings and serve.

## Notes

- The creator says the protein powder is optional but recommends it for sweetness. If omitting it, use less yoghurt; the source does not specify the adjusted amount.
- Do not add all the liquid at once. Stop when the mixture is thick and spoonable.
- The source writes “30–40 milk or water” without a unit. The quantity has been preserved rather than assigning an inferred unit.
- The source presents one serving bowl but does not state a formal yield.
- This is a no-bake brownie-batter-style dessert, not a recipe for baked brownies.
