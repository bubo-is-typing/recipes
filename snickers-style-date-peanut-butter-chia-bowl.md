# Snickers-Style Date and Peanut Butter Chia Bowl

*A creamy overnight chia pudding with mashed dates, peanut butter and Greek yoghurt.*

## Ingredients

### Pudding — 1 large serving

- 35 g pitted dates
- Boiling water, to soften the dates (quantity not specified)
- 20 g smooth peanut butter
- 100 g high-protein Greek yoghurt
- 25 g chia seeds
- 100 ml high-protein oat milk

### Optional topping

- Melted chocolate, to taste (quantity not specified)

## Method

1. Put the dates into a heatproof bowl or container, add boiling water and mash the dates until softened and broken down. Some small date pieces can remain.
2. Stir in the peanut butter, Greek yoghurt, chia seeds and oat milk until thoroughly combined.
3. Refrigerate overnight, or for at least a few hours, until thick and creamy.
4. Serve as it is, or add the optional melted chocolate suggested in the description.
